Changes are intentionally limited to WaveSpeed credential resilience inside the Cloudflare Worker:
- multiple server-side WaveSpeed secrets are supported;
- submit and result polling fail over to the next key on credential/quota/rate-limit/server/network failures;
- polling can locate a task created under another configured key/account;
- invalid payload/model errors do not waste requests across every key;
- health exposes configured key count only, never secret values.

- WAVESPEED_API_KEY

- ... up to WAVESPEED_API_KEY_10
